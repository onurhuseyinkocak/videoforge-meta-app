Privacy Policy

VideoForge Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Onarika ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the VideoForge application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you access and use our application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Meta Ads Account Data

When you authorize VideoForge to access your Meta (Facebook) account, we collect the following information:

  • Campaign performance data (impressions, clicks, conversions)
  • Ad creative information and content
  • Advertising spend and budget data
  • Account identifiers and basic account information
  • Audience targeting parameters

This data is accessed through Meta's official Graph API using the ads_management and ads_read permissions. We only access data that you explicitly authorize through the OAuth 2.0 login process.

2.2 Session Data

We may collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps during your use of the App for security and operational purposes.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Enabling advertising campaign management and optimization
  • Displaying analytics and performance metrics for your campaigns
  • Providing recommendations for campaign improvement
  • Authenticating and authorizing your account access
  • Maintaining security and detecting fraudulent activity

4. Data Retention

Meta Ads account data is retrieved on-demand during your active session with VideoForge. We do not permanently store Meta advertising data in our databases. Data may be cached temporarily in memory during your session to improve performance and user experience, but such cached data is discarded when your session ends.

Session logs and technical data may be retained for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting purposes.

5. Data Sharing with Third Parties

VideoForge does not share your Meta Ads account data with any third parties. Your data is used exclusively by Onarika to provide the App's services to you. The only exception is the Meta Ads API itself, which is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.—your data must flow through Meta's infrastructure to retrieve your account information.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to any third party.

6. Meta Platform Integration

VideoForge integrates with Meta's official APIs. Your use of this integration is also governed by:

  • Meta's Platform Policies: https://developers.facebook.com/policy/
  • Meta's Data Policy: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/

You authorize us to request access to specific Meta permissions (ads_management, ads_read) on your behalf. You may revoke this access at any time through your Meta account settings.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted transmission of data over HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication with Meta
  • Access controls and authentication checks
  • Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. GDPR Compliance

8.1 Legal Basis

For users in the European Union and other jurisdictions with equivalent data protection laws, we process your data based on your explicit consent. By authorizing VideoForge to access your Meta account, you consent to the processing of your advertising account data as described in this Privacy Policy.

8.2 Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (by revoking API access)
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data)
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

To exercise these rights or withdraw consent, contact us at info@onarika.net.

8.3 Data Processor

When processing data on your behalf as an advertising campaign manager, Onarika acts as a data processor. You, the advertiser, are the data controller. We process data only under your instructions and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where required, by seeking your renewed consent.
